Small Team Sessions

The small version works better

Most people ask us about the big one: a full buyout, a couple of hundred guests, the whole club. The small version is easier to book and better to be at. Eight people fill two courts, everyone is on court for the whole session, and nobody stands on the side waiting for a turn. Padel is doubles only, underarm serve, and the walls keep the ball alive, so beginners rally instead of chasing.

Team Session
Up to 8
Two courts, two hours, one coach for the whole session. Racquets and balls provided. Nobody needs to have held a racquet before, and everybody is playing real points within half an hour.
Two Teams
Up to 16
All four courts. Two departments against each other, or one group split into a round-robin so every pair plays every other pair. Same format, twice the people, still nobody sitting out.
Standing Slot
Monthly or quarterly
The same group, the same slot, already on the calendar. Weekday off-peak hours are the easiest for us to hold and the easiest for a team to keep.
Client Entertaining
Small groups
Two courts and the upstairs lounge for a client group or a board dinner. Quieter than a buyout, still coached, still ends with a drink upstairs.
